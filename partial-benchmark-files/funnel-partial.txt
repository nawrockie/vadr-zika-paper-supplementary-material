Part A finding:
  The VADR 2020 paper (Schaffer et al., BMC Bioinformatics 21:211) does NOT define a
  universal minimum-length threshold for "partial" (EPN's recollected ">200nt" does not
  appear anywhere in the paper). Instead, in "Obtaining norovirus and dengue virus
  sequences" the paper derives a VIRUS-SPECIFIC empirical length threshold per taxon
  from GenBank's own "complete genome" annotation label distribution (>7380nt for
  norovirus, >10,371nt for dengue virus). This project's existing >=10267nt ZIKV cutoff
  (95% of RefSeq NC_035889's 10808nt length) is methodologically the same kind of
  per-virus empirical "complete genome" threshold, just derived via EB's original 2025
  recipe rather than re-derived here. Operationalization used below: everything below
  the existing 10267nt cutoff (after the same SYN/PAT division strip, munus the 8
  current-CM training accessions, minus non-genuine PDB-structure "sequence" records)
  is treated as the "partial sequence" pool. No additional universal length floor was
  applied, consistent with the paper not using one.
